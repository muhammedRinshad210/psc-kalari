About the Project

About PSC Kalari

PSC Kalari is an independent, non-commercial educational project created to help Kerala Public Service Commission aspirants prepare for government examinations with accurate practice materials.

കേരള പി.എസ്.സി പരീക്ഷാർത്ഥികൾക്കായി സൗജന്യവും പരസ്യരഹിതവുമായി തയ്യാറാക്കിയ പഠന സഹായി.

Who Makes PSC Kalari

PSC Kalari is developed and maintained by a dedicated team of Kerala PSC educators, competitive exam mentors, and open-web enthusiasts based in Kerala. Our goal is to make high-quality exam preparation accessible to every candidate across Kerala regardless of economic background or coaching institute access.

Free and Open Access (No Login Required)

The entire platform is 100% free with no login, no accounts, no subscriptions, and no advertisements. We respect candidate privacy: your daily study streaks, mock scores, notebook bookmarks, and missed questions are saved securely on your own device using standard browser storage (localStorage). No personal data or phone numbers are ever collected.

Question Review Status

All questions on PSC Kalari are original formulations written from authoritative primary references: the Constitution of India, official Kerala Gazetteers, SCERT and NCERT textbooks, Census of India data, and the Kerala Economic Review. Questions are being checked against official sources, and you can report any mistake.

How to Report a Mistake

Content integrity is our top priority. Every question card on the practice screen, the results review sheet, and the search screen includes a visible "Report a mistake" button. Clicking this button opens a feedback form with the specific question ID automatically prefilled. Disputed facts or typos reported by candidates are cross-checked against official publications.
